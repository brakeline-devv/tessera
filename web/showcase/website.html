Why only Tuesdays

One day a week, the whole room smells of fire.

We could roast every day. We'd rather roast once, carefully, and sell out.

Roasting weekly keeps our batches small and our stock honest. Nothing sits in a warehouse. If a coffee runs out on Friday, it's gone until the next Tuesday, and we're fine with that. Freshness isn't a selling point for us; it's the schedule.

Good questions

Before you brew.

How fresh will my coffee be when it arrives?

Subscriptions roast on Tuesday and ship Thursday, so mainland deliveries usually land Friday or Saturday, three to four days off roast. That's right in the sweet spot for filter, and espresso is at its best from around day seven.

Can I pause or skip a month?

Of course. Reply to any shipping email before Monday at noon and we'll skip that roast. Pausing or cancelling never costs anything.

Do you sell wholesale to cafés and restaurants?

In small quantities, yes. We supply a handful of places around Lisbon and only take on new partners when a Tuesday has room. Write to us at ola@ember.pt.

Is the packaging recyclable?

Our bags are home-compostable kraft with a plant-based lining. The one-way valve should go in the plastic bin. Subscribers collecting at the café can bring their own tins and skip the bag entirely.
